# C1 blockout provenance

Q and E sequences/montages are generated by `UU03AnimationEditor::BuildC1Assets` in the isolated C1 clone. The authoring source derives procedural arm/wrist keys from the accepted U03 A1 diagonal/overhead authoring, using the existing U01 skeleton, carry and grip poses with their original provenance. Q uses .30/.40/.80 deadlines; E uses .25/.35/.75. These are original procedural blockout variations, not purchased or third-party combat clips.

Montages have named Startup/Active/Recovery sections, fixed roots and no gameplay notifies. Runtime seeks to authority time and ignores root motion. Feet-based collision and motor remain independent of clips. HUD punctuation and text distinguish phases, `< FEINT >` and `// INTERRUPTED //` without relying on color. Product art and feel remain unaccepted.
